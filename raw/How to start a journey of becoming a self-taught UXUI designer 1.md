Before you start reading, I want you to know that I am still on my way to landing my first job. So, it is not a guide from a pro. I just want to share my interesting journey with valuable sources for people like me, who have begun to learn UI/UX design or planning to do so.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/0*o9vRbaxoJzAUevS6)

## Why did I decide to study UI/UX design?

In 2020 I started thinking about changing my profession. By that time, I was working in the ministry of finance and studying comparative private and international law. Even though I was good at it, I just felt like my potential and creativity were locked in a cage, and I could not pursue my dreams.

During the first lockdown, [**Coursera**](https://www.coursera.org/) made courses free of charge. I figured that it was my chance to find a better fit for me. I took many courses, and one of them was [**Design Principles: an Introduction**](https://www.coursera.org/learn/design-principles?specialization=interaction-design#syllabus) by Scott Klemmer. That\`s when I got interested.

After a year or so, I quit my job, obtain a master\`s degree, and traveled solo in Europe. When I came back in late October, I was ready to invest my time, energy, and mental power in this career transition. I started the journey of becoming a UI/UX designer.

## Prepare to start

I was used to traditional education, so self-education was a little bit overwhelming. I had to create a kind-of syllabus, and I had to plan the whole process. For planning and focusing, I used the following tools:

- [**Pomofocus**](https://pomofocus.io/) is a timer with the Pomodoro technique. I used it to focus on the tasks and don\`t burn out. (in fact, I am using it while writing this article)
- [**Todoist**](https://todoist.com/) is a good way to create tasks and plan your day. As an alternative, I sometimes used a pen and notebook.

### How to start

To be honest, the hardest part of my journey was doing UI.  
I always felt that my work was very amateur. It was overwhelming seeing all those cool dribble shots and comparing them to my work. I struggled a lot, even if I knew that after a few years of practice, I will be a much better UI designer than I am today.

- **Create a copy of a website**

The biggest mistake I\`ve made was that I started by NOT copying others. After a few weeks of struggle, I changed this approach. You can use the inspect tool to copy pixel by pixel.

- **Do the challenges**

There are dozens of different challenges on the internet. Currently, I am doing a daily UI challenge. For 100 days you will receive daily challenges in your email. Many UI designers [criticize](https://uxdesign.cc/daily-ui-c09c8ea76622) this challenge, and I partially agree with them. On the other hand, as a beginner, it is very good to practice every day and this challenge is one way to do so.
