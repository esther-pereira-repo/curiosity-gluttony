**Derrick Jensen** is an American [environmental activist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_movement "Environmental movement"), author and [ecophilosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecophilosopher "Ecophilosopher") [^1] in the [anarcho-primitivist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anarcho-primitivist "Anarcho-primitivist") tradition.[^2] [^3] He believes that human civilization is and has always been unsustainable for the planet.[^4]

## Early life

Derrick Jensen was born in 1960 [^5] in [Nebraska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebraska "Nebraska") and was raised in Colorado.[^6] He attended the [Colorado School of Mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_School_of_Mines "Colorado School of Mines") with a scholarship and graduated with a [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor%27s_degree "Bachelor's degree") in mineral engineering and physics. Even before graduating, he intended to become a writer. Jensen worked briefly in engineering and as a small commercial beekeeper. He became ill from [Crohn's disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crohn%27s_disease "Crohn's disease") and had a slow recovery. His experience with the disease contributed to his approach towards Western culture. In 1991, Jensen received a [Master of Fine Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Fine_Arts "Master of Fine Arts") degree in creative writing from [Eastern Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Washington_University "Eastern Washington University").[^5]

## Writing and activism

> We don't think about the violence that makes the comforts and elegancies of our way of life happen. The world is being destroyed before our eyes, and it's really not that hard to tell the truth about it. It doesn't take that much courage.

— Derrick Jensen [^5]

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/80/JensenKeithOccupyOakland2011.png/250px-JensenKeithOccupyOakland2011.png)

Lierre Keith (left) and Jensen (right) with Deep Green Resistance at Occupy Oakland in 2011

Jensen contends that human civilization is and has always been unsustainable for the planet.[^7] His writing and philosophy centers on the destructive tendencies of Western culture and how it proliferates between people and generations. His *The Culture of Make Believe*, a nonlinear composite of memoir and philosophy, outlines how Western culture was built upon brutality. Jensen has said this approach comes from wanting to be honest and "forthright without whining". When writing about massacres, slavery, and industrial forestry, he balances statistics and personal stories to "mix analysis with blood".[^5]

He wrote for two environmental organizations in 1995. A commission from an environmental organization led to his co-authored *Railroads and Clearcuts*, and [Sierra Club Books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Club_Books "Sierra Club Books") published his interviews with activists, *Listening to the Land*. Jensen began *[A Language Older Than Words](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Language_Older_Than_Words "A Language Older Than Words")* in 1996, published by [Context Books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Context_Books "Context Books") in 2000. He described the book as beginning a trilogy, starting with how to be "sane in a crazy culture", followed by what one sees by becoming sane (*The Culture of Make Believe*), followed by what to do about what one sees.[^5]

Jensen is a [critic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_critic "Cultural critic") of the mainstream environmental movement's focus on preserving civilization and technology over preserving the natural world.[^8] He promotes [civil disobedience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_disobedience "Civil disobedience"), radical activism, and dismantling infrastructure on a massive level in order to halt what he has called "the murder of the planet".[^8]

[Lierre Keith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lierre_Keith "Lierre Keith") and Jensen co-founded [Deep Green Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_Green_Resistance "Deep Green Resistance") in 2011.[^8] His belief, and the organization's position, that women-only spaces should [exclude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feminist_views_on_transgender_topics "Feminist views on transgender topics") [trans women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans_women "Trans women") has drawn criticism.[^8] [^9]

### Reception

*[Utne Reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utne_Reader "Utne Reader")* named Jensen among "50 Visionaries Who Are Changing the World" in 2008,[^10] and *[Democracy Now!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy_Now! "Democracy Now!")* says that he "has been called the poet-philosopher of the [ecology movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology_movement "Ecology movement") ".[^11]

## Personal life

Jensen lives in [Crescent City, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crescent_City,_California "Crescent City, California"), and has cats.[^5]
