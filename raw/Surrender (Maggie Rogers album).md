***Surrender*** is the second studio album by American indie pop singer-songwriter [Maggie Rogers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maggie_Rogers "Maggie Rogers"), released on July 29, 2022, by Debay Sounds and [Capitol Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Records "Capitol Records"). It follows the release of her debut studio album, *[Heard It in a Past Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heard_It_in_a_Past_Life "Heard It in a Past Life")* (2019).

The album features a couple of contributions from notable singers. The track "Shatter" features [Florence Welch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Welch "Florence Welch") of [Florence and the Machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_and_the_Machine "Florence and the Machine") providing additional vocals and playing tambourine, and "I've Got a Friend" features [Clairo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clairo "Clairo") and [Claud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claud_\(singer\) "Claud (singer)") speaking.

The trailer for the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") series *[XO, Kitty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XO,_Kitty "XO, Kitty")* featured "Want Want" from this album.[^11] [^12]

## Background and promotion

Rogers teased recording sessions for her forthcoming second studio album throughout 2021 into early 2022. She stated that the album would be self-produced and sounded like "feral joy", which is what she would later name her tour in support of the album.[^13] In March 2022, Rogers shared a link to a site counting down to March 30. On that day, she announced that her second studio album, titled *Surrender*, would be released on July 29, 2022.[^14] Rogers simultaneously released an album trailer she co-directed with Michael Scanlon.[^15] On June 15, 2022, Rogers revealed the album's track listing via [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram").[^16]

## Composition

*Surrender* is an [indie pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_pop "Indie pop"),[^7] [alternative pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_rock#Alternative_pop "Alternative rock"),[^10] and [electropop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electropop "Electropop") [^5] album with elements of [alternative rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_rock "Alternative rock"),[^3] dance,[^17] [Americana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americana_\(music\) "Americana (music)"),[^7] [new wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_wave_music "New wave music"),[^6] [electroacoustic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electroacoustic_music "Electroacoustic music"),[^3] [slacker rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slacker_rock "Slacker rock"),[^4] [synth-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synth-pop "Synth-pop"),[^10] [folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_music "Folk music"),[^5] [glam rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glam_rock "Glam rock"),[^17] and [sunshine pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunshine_pop "Sunshine pop").[^7]
